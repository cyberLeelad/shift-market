# Shift Manager

## User journey (Beta)

Fionna, a desk staff member, opens Shift Market and enters her kerberos. The calendar shows the mail desk’s weekly shifts, with 2-hour blocks from 8am to midnight. She creates a weekly shift for Mondays through a date range. She then claims one of the open shifts, and its block changes to show her name. When Cake tries to claim the same shift, she is unable to, so the shift stays with Fionna.
